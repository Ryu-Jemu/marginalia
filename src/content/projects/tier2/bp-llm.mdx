Ray-tracing output for the DeepMIMO o1_28 scenario at 28 GHz goes through a
Saleh-Valenzuela channel over three paths, gets beamed against a 32-point DFT
codebook, and the arg-max beam per position becomes the label. That produced
84,019 sliding windows of forty past steps against ten future
ones.<N id="bp-llm-data" /> Beams are predicted every 16 ms.

The model is a frozen GPT-2 with three trainable stages around it — patch
embedding with reversible normalisation, cross-variable attention over beam
index and departure angle, and reprogramming onto 256 text prototypes — plus a
prompt prefix carrying domain knowledge, the instruction and the series
statistics. 339,338 parameters move; the backbone does not.<N id="bp-llm-training" />

It trained. There is no held-out split — the normalisation statistics and the
loss come from the same indices — so the curve says the model fitted what it saw
and nothing about whether it predicts beams. The evaluation that produced the
survey's beamforming gains was a separate run.
