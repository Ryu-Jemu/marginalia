A prosecutor and a defender each argue over three rounds, and each is fed by its
own researcher, so the two sides arrive with different evidence rather than
different readings of one set. A judge weighs the exchange and can send either
researcher back for more, bounded by explicit termination conditions so a hard
claim cannot loop indefinitely.

"Cannot determine" is a verdict the system is allowed to return. The final
confidence figure is computed in Python rather than produced by the
model,<N id="examiner-determinism" /> and citations are verified against the
retrieved documents in code rather than only forbidden in the prompt.
