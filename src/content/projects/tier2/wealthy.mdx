A survey of people in their twenties put compound interest lowest of the
financial concepts we tested. Five candidate products were scored against market
size, competition and whether we could demonstrate anything within a semester;
one survived.

Fed the same question repeatedly, the prototype's answers drifted, so the deck
ends on the distance between a demo and a service — response consistency, cost
per answer, and where the numbers would have to come from.
