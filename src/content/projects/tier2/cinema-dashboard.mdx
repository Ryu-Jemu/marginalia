The question was not whether multiplex revenue fell — eighteen years of the
national box-office series answer that on their own, with a peak in 2019 and a
collapse to an eighth of it two years later.<N id="cinema-fall" /> The question
was what else moved with it.

The premise the team started from was that a chain's share of a region follows
how many screens it has there. It does correlate — 0.68 for the largest
operator, 0.57 and 0.55 for the other two — and at eight regions not one of
those coefficients clears the usual threshold.<N id="cinema-correlation" /> Per
capita consumption against share behaves identically. A positive coefficient
that cannot be distinguished from noise is a finding about the sample, and
reporting it as support would have been the easy edit.

The part I would rather talk about is collection. A Selenium crawler against a
site that kept changing its markup produced a three-byte file one morning and
nobody noticed until the numbers looked odd. Rewriting it against a documented
search API made the pipeline dull and repeatable, which is what rebuilding the
whole thing in one command actually requires.
