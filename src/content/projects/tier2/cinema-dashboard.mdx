**Time-series analysis** aggregated revenue and admissions by year for 2004–2021. In the collected dataset, 2021 revenue was approximately one eighth of the 2019 peak.<N id="cinema-fall" />

**Regional analysis** found screen-count versus market-share correlations of approximately 0.68, 0.57 and 0.55 across the three chains. None was significant at p < 0.05 in the eight-region sample.<N id="cinema-correlation" /> The positive correlation between per-capita consumption and market share was also non-significant in this sample.

**Collection improvement** addressed a Selenium crawler that depended on changing page markup and produced only a three-byte file. I replaced it with a documented search API and configured a single collection command.
