import N from '../../components/note/N.astro';

Public datasets were cleaned and joined on Seoul's district key before fitting
an OLS model. Across twenty-five districts, the five-variable model explains
about 84% of the observed variation in crime counts.<N id="crime-model" />
Population and entertainment-venue counts are significant in this fit; CCTV
count is not.<N id="crime-significant" /> This cross-sectional association does
not establish the effect of installing CCTV or the causes of crime.

Correlation and variance inflation were checked before interpreting the
coefficients. Low VIF values indicate limited collinearity among the included
variables; they do not rule out omitted variables or establish unbiased causal
estimates. Streetlight counts use an estimate rather than a directly observed
district dataset.<N id="crime-streetlight" /> Cross-validated Lasso and Ridge
provided additional comparisons of coefficients and model fit.

The residual map compares observed and fitted counts by district. The model
over-predicts Seongdong by 1,862 crimes and under-predicts Mapo by
1,387.<N id="crime-residuals" /> Daytime and floating population are candidates
for a follow-up analysis; their contribution to these residuals was not measured.
