import N from '../../components/note/N.astro';

I modelled pricing for two network slices using a subscription fee, an included allowance and an overage rate. The simulation links prices to customer churn, so each pricing decision affects both current revenue and future demand.

**Controlled comparisons** showed greater PPO revenue uplift as churn sensitivity increased. The difference at m=1 was not statistically significant.<N id="rl-uplift" /> Myopic-PPO, trained with a zero discount factor to consider only immediate rewards, did not differ significantly from the max-price baseline at any tested setting.<N id="rl-myopic" /> This supports the interpretation that accounting for future rewards contributes to the advantage.

**Slice-level analysis** attributed 97% of the m=3 revenue gap to eMBB.<N id="rl-embb-attribution" /> The aggregate result therefore reflects a gain concentrated in this slice.

**Algorithm comparison at m=1** showed lower returns for TD3 than Max-Price, while the PPO–SAC difference was not significant.<N id="rl-algorithms" /> I wrote the paper as first author and received a silver award in the conference paper competition.<N id="rl-award" />
