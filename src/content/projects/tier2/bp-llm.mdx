Ray-tracing output at 28 GHz, reduced to the strongest path's departure angles,
windowed into past-40 and future-10 sequences. A frozen GPT-2 with a patch
embedding in front of it, cross-attention reprogramming, and a prompt prefix,
trained end to end on the small set of parameters that remained
unfrozen.<N id="bp-llm-training" />

It trained. That is the entire honest claim. There is no held-out split and no
metric, so the loss curve says the model fitted what it saw and nothing about
whether it predicts beams. I am listing it because the preprocessing and the
reprogramming were real work, and because leaving out the missing half would
make it look like a result.
