A survey of people in their twenties found the lowest understanding for compound interest among the financial concepts tested. We evaluated five product candidates by market size, competition and feasibility within one semester, then selected one.

The prototype returned inconsistent answers to repeated questions. The presentation identified response consistency and cost per answer as areas requiring further validation.
