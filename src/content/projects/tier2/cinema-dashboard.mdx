The finding that survived scrutiny was locational: among the twenty
highest-revenue branches, one operator had a single shopping-mall site where a
competitor had ten. The hypothesis the team started with — that branch count
explained the gap — did not survive the numbers.

The part I would rather talk about is the scraper. A Selenium crawler against a
site that kept changing its markup produced a three-byte CSV one morning and
nobody noticed until the numbers looked odd. Rewriting it against a documented
search API made the pipeline dull and repeatable, which is what a one-command
rebuild of the whole dashboard actually requires.
