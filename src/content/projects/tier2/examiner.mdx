A prosecutor and a defender receive evidence from separate researcher agents and present arguments over three rounds. A judge agent evaluates the arguments and can request further retrieval. Explicit round limits and termination conditions bound the process.

"Cannot determine" is a verdict the system is allowed to return. The final
confidence figure is computed in Python rather than produced by the
model.<N id="examiner-determinism" /> Citations are checked against retrieved documents in code.
