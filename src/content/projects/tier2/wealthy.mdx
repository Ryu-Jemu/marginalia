A survey of people in their twenties put compound interest lowest of the
financial concepts we tested, which is what pointed the idea at explanation
rather than at portfolio tools. Five candidate products were scored against
market size, competition and whether we could demonstrate anything within a
semester; one survived.

The slide I would keep is the last one. We fed the prototype the same question
repeatedly and the answers drifted, so the deck ends with the distance between a
demo and a service — response consistency, cost per answer, and where the
numbers would have to come from — rather than with a mock-up presented as a
product.
