**Data preparation** used the DeepMIMO o1_28 scenario at 28 GHz, a three-path Saleh-Valenzuela channel and a 32-point DFT codebook. The highest-response beam at each position became the label. Preprocessing produced 84,019 windows with 40 input steps and 10 target steps.<N id="bp-llm-data" />

**Model implementation** combines patch embedding, reversible normalisation and cross-variable attention over beam indices and departure angles. Inputs are reprogrammed into 256 text prototypes and combined with a prompt carrying domain knowledge, instructions and sequence statistics. GPT-2 remains frozen while 339,338 parameters are trained.<N id="bp-llm-training" />

**Validation scope** is limited to the recorded training run. Normalisation and loss calculation use the same sample indices, with no held-out split or evaluation metrics. The training curve therefore does not establish prediction accuracy.
